# Social Engineering

This section summarizes the course report's discussion of attacks that manipulate people into disclosing information or taking unsafe actions.

## Techniques covered
- Phishing and spear phishing
- Vishing (voice/phone-based scams)
- Pretexting
- Baiting

## Defensive practices
- Verify the sender and destination URL before acting.
- Confirm urgent requests through a separate, trusted channel.
- Never disclose passwords or authentication codes to callers or in email.
- Use multi-factor authentication and unique passwords.
- Provide recurring security-awareness training.
